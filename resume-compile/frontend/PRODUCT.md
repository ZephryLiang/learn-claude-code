# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese-speaking job seekers (domestic and overseas) who are actively optimizing their resumes. They have a target job description open, want to analyze gaps between their resume and the JD, get an assessment of their interview chances, receive a concrete remediation roadmap, and rewrite their resume with narrative storytelling. They are technically literate (comfortable editing LaTeX) but their primary need is AI-powered insight, not manual formatting.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered resume workspace that helps job seekers understand exactly where they stand relative to a target role and what to do about it. Success is: user uploads a resume, pastes a JD, and walks away with a clear gap analysis, a prioritized learning roadmap, and a rewritten resume that tells a compelling story. The editor (LaTeX/Monaco) is the means; AI analysis and actionable output is the end.

## Brand Personality

Professional, sharp, incisive — cuts to the core of what matters. Explains the "why" behind every recommendation (not just surface-level suggestions). The product is a confident career consultant, not a bureaucratic HR tool: authoritative but not stuffy, precise but not cold.

## Anti-references

- Dated enterprise software (gray tables, heavy borders, cluttered toolbars)
- Over-minimalist "SaaS template" look (giant hero metrics, generic gradients, Inter font defaults)
- Cookie-cutter AI product aesthetic (purple gradients, glassmorphism, sparkle icons)

## Design Principles

- **Professional credibility** — Every pixel conveys authority. No decorative fluff, no gimmicks. The UI earns trust through precision and restraint, not through visual noise.
- **直击核心 (Straight to the core)** — The most important information is immediately visible. Secondary controls are available but never in the way. Every screen has one clear focal point.
- **知其所以然 (Explain the why)** — AI outputs show reasoning, not just results. The UI reveals the chain of thought: "here's what we found, here's why it matters, here's what to do." Users learn, not just receive.
- **锐利而不冷漠 (Sharp but not cold)** — Professional doesn't mean sterile. Use deliberate color, refined texture, and precise typography to create a workspace that feels alive and engaging, not merely functional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance target
- Support for reduced motion preferences
- Color palette that works for common forms of color blindness
- Chinese-first UI with full i18n support
